The nights are getting longer, the fog rolls in earlier, and somewhere between the trees stands a thin, black figure that does not take its eyes off you. From **24 October 2026**, *Slender* returns to our server, this time as a reworked Halloween round.
<!--more-->

## A look back and a fresh start

In 2023 we brought *Slender: The Eight Pages* to Minecraft as an asymmetric multiplayer mode. The idea worked, but balancing, rushed maps and technical problems clouded the rounds. Three years later we are bringing the mode back in a reworked version.

At the start of each round one player is picked at random as **Slender** and hunts everyone else. All other players are **Survivors** and have to collect pages. How many pages are needed depends on the number of players in the round.

- **The Survivors** have to collect all pages of the round together before Slender takes them out. Sprinting costs stamina: once the bar is empty, you cannot run, so pace yourselves.
- **Slender** has to take out every Survivor before they have all the pages.

## Before you join

- When you join, the server asks you to accept the **resource pack**. It is mandatory: if you decline, the server kicks you.
- The GUI is kept dark on purpose, so opening your inventory mid-round does not break the mood.
- New effects run through the game's shaders and are controlled by the server.

Rounds run from **24 October 2026, 18:00**, to **2 November 2026, 23:59** (Berlin time). All roles, rules and details are on the [Slender event page](/en/events/slender). The page [Join the server](/en/join) shows how to connect.

## The website in its Halloween costume

Our website also puts on a different outfit for this period. From 20 October to 2 November (by the Berlin calendar) the colours shift to violet, orange and green, in light and dark mode alike, and the logo and favicon are swapped. A subtle decoration lies over the pages: two spider webs in the upper corners and, from medium screen widths up, up to three slowly flying bats. The bats stay hidden when the system asks for reduced motion, and the decoration can be clicked through. Outside this period the site keeps its standard design. To preview it in advance, add `?season=halloween` to a page address.

For preparation, the event page holds the guides for Survivors and Slender. Questions about the round are welcome on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/onelitefeather).

We look forward to seeing you. Stick together and find the pages!
