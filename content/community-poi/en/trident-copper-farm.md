## What is this?

The Trident / Copper Farm is a finished farm tower by Svenko1. The tall tower stands with its base in water in the middle of a forest and carries a widely overhanging roof slab on top. Inside, a glass walkway leads through the water to the farm chambers, where mobs sit behind panes of glass.
