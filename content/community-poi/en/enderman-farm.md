## What is this?

The Enderman Farm is a finished farm tower in the End. A tower of dark bricks rises above a round platform with a purple pattern, carries a huge Eye of Ender at its centre and ends in a glowing crown of end stone. At the foot of the tower there is an enchanting corner with bookshelves and a long row of chests for the loot.

The screenshots were taken by Svenko1 and B3nNy.

## Pay a visit

- Coordinates: X 260 / Y 57 / Z -50 (the End)
- The farm is finished – please don't rebuild any part of it
