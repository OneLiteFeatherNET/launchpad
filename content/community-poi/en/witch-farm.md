## What is this?

The Witch Farm by Cainpain234 is a tall, narrow tower in a wooded landscape. At its top sits a chamber made of glass and wood, with a small figure standing inside. Yellow scaffolds run along the tower down to the ground.

## Pay a visit

- Coordinates: X 1032 / Y 174 / Z 4742 (overworld)
- The tower is visible from afar and rises above the treetops
- The farm is finished – please don't rebuild any part of it
