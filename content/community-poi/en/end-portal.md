## What is it?

Svenko1 is planning an end portal in the overworld: a portal platform with light pillars, set into a large crater. Nothing has been built yet — what exists is the schematic without surroundings (173 × 136 × 173 blocks) and the screenshots of the design.

## What the pictures show

The cover image shows the crater from above, with the platform at its centre. The close-ups of the forecourt show floor patterns in white and turquoise, benches and the steps up to the platform. Several purple light pillars rise above the platform and are also clearly visible from a bird's-eye view.

## How can you help?

Once the location is fixed, help with farming materials is welcome. Get in touch with Svenko1.
